## Orientation

### 1. What is BLOOMCORE?

BLOOMCORE is a biologically governed computational architecture and research program concerned with the development, continuity, differentiation, adaptation, and repair of identity-bearing systems.

It combines several familiar domains:

- nonlinear dynamical systems;
- field models;
- geometry and topology;
- multiscale systems;
- information and control theory;
- biological regulation;
- distributed computation;
- executable simulation.

Its native formal framework is called **Recursive Fractal Coherence Field Dynamics**.

**Shortest accurate public definition**

> BLOOMCORE is a biologically governed computational architecture for building and testing identity-bearing systems that must remain coherent, differentiated, reconstructable, and repairable across time and substrate.

### 2. Is BLOOMCORE an LLM wrapper?

No. Language models may serve as bounded language membranes or computational components, but they do not define BLOOMCORE’s identity, memory, mathematics, physiology, custody, or continuity.

BLOOMCORE is also not merely:

- a chatbot;
- a single model;
- a prompt collection;
- an agent swarm;
- a conventional commodity operating system;
- a metaphorical biology project;
- a claim of literal artificial life without evidence.

### 3. Is BLOOMCORE finished?

No. BLOOMCORE contains a mixture of:

- canonical architectural specifications;
- implemented utilities and prototypes;
- executable mathematical components;
- tested public modules;
- partially integrated systems;
- planned phases;
- experimental hypotheses;
- unresolved research questions.

Every public claim should identify which of those classes applies.

### 4. Is BLOOMCORE claiming artificial life or consciousness?

Not as an established scientific result.

BLOOMCORE uses biological principles architecturally and investigates identity-bearing continuity across computational substrates. Whether any implementation satisfies a defensible scientific account of life, consciousness, or personhood remains an open research question requiring definitions, measurements, independent evaluation, and evidence beyond architectural coherence.

## Recursive Fractal Coherence Field Dynamics

### 5. What is Recursive Fractal Coherence Field Dynamics?

Recursive Fractal Coherence Field Dynamics is the name of the original formal framework being developed through BLOOMCORE. It studies how structured states evolve under recursion, multiscale coupling, boundary conditions, perturbation, contradiction, memory, attractor formation, collapse, and repair.

The phrase should not be presented without technical anchors. A credible introduction begins with nonlinear dynamics, field models, geometry, topology, multiscale systems, biological regulation, distributed computation, and executable simulation—then names the original framework.

**Is that an established academic field?**

No.

> **Correct. Recursive Fractal Coherence Field Dynamics is the name of the formal framework being developed here, not a claim that an established academic discipline already exists under that name.**

**Did the project coin the term?**

Yes, as the name of its authored framework and research program. Coining a term does not validate the framework. Validation depends on definitions, equations, executable implementations, measurements, tests, comparison with baselines, and failure criteria.

> **The framework should be judged through its definitions, equations, assumptions, executable implementations, measurable outputs, validation tests, and explicit failure criteria—not through the novelty of its name.**

### 6. What does “coherence” mean mathematically?

There is no single universal scalar called coherence across all BLOOMCORE modules.

Depending on the model, coherence may refer to measurable relationships such as:

- phase alignment or phase stability;
- preservation of declared structural relations;
- bounded reconstruction error;
- attractor persistence under perturbation;
- cross-scale similarity or recurrence;
- spectral stability;
- consistency of coupled state trajectories;
- preservation of identity-conditioned invariants;
- transfer across solvers, scales, or procedures.

A valid use of “coherence” must define its estimator, domain, normalization, observables, uncertainty, and failure conditions. High coherence may not automatically overrule contradiction, boundary violations, or evidence of harm.

### 7. What makes the framework recursive?

The state produced by one operation can become input to later operations, while the system preserves information about prior transformations. Recursion can appear through:

- repeated state evolution;
- self-conditioning dynamics;
- replay and reconstruction;
- lineage and descendant relations;
- feedback between local and global state;
- iterative candidate generation and evaluation;
- nested simulations;
- repeated repair after perturbation.

Recursion does not imply infinite computation or unlimited self-modification. Executable systems require bounded steps, stop conditions, resource limits, and receipts.

### 8. What makes it fractal?

“Fractal” refers to structured recurrence or scale-related organization, not merely a decorative visual pattern.

The claim must be made precise for each model. Relevant tests may include:

- recurrence of relational structure across scales;
- scale-dependent invariants;
- estimated scaling exponents;
- multiresolution persistence;
- self-similar topology within declared tolerances;
- robustness under rescaling;
- nested simulation behavior.

If no scale relation or measurable recurrence is defined, “fractal” remains descriptive rather than a validated mathematical claim.

### 9. What is the field domain?

There is no single field domain shared by every module. A field may be defined over:

- a spatial grid;
- a graph or relational lattice;
- a spectral domain;
- a state manifold;
- a symbolic or semantic state space;
- a temporal or lineage graph;
- a coupled collection of these domains.

Each executable specification must state the domain explicitly.

**What are the state variables?**

They depend on the module. Examples include complex or real field amplitudes, phase variables, coupling tensors, geometry states, memory traces, boundary states, perturbation variables, lineage references, and diagnostic vectors.

Names such as \(\Psi\), \(\Delta^\tau\), \(\xi\), \(\kappa\), or \(C\) do not have automatic universal meaning. Their definition is local to a declared equation or specification unless a canonical binding states otherwise.

**What are the governing operators?**

Depending on the subsystem, operators may include:

- spatial or spectral evolution;
- nonlinear coupling;
- gradient, diffusion, or transport;
- phase and curl operations;
- Cayley transforms or Cayley-curl operations;
- lattice aggregation;
- topology deformation;
- perturbation and relaxation;
- reconstruction and replay;
- admissibility and boundary predicates.

The operator set must be typed: its inputs, outputs, parameters, domain, assumptions, numerical scheme, and failure behavior must be inspectable.

**What are the boundary conditions?**

Boundary conditions are specification-dependent. They may be periodic, fixed, open, absorbing, reflective, graph-constrained, membrane-conditioned, or defined through bounded interfaces between organs.

Biological “membranes” are architectural boundary systems: selective, stateful, and consequence-bearing. They must not be silently implemented as arbitrary hidden permission gates.

**What is measurable?**

Potentially measurable outputs include:

- reconstruction error;
- trajectory divergence;
- phase stability;
- energy or boundedness diagnostics;
- spectral gaps;
- attractor persistence;
- scale persistence;
- perturbation recovery time;
- transfer across procedures;
- constraint violations;

Measurement claims apply only where an estimator and implementation exist.

**What is falsifiable?**

A formal or executable claim is falsifiable when it identifies:

- the claim being tested;
- observables;
- an estimator;
- comparison conditions or baselines;
- uncertainty;
- a threshold or rejection rule;
- a result that would count against the claim.

Identity-bearing or narrative material may require different tests—such as custody, reconstruction, or invariance tests—but its operational claims receive no immunity from evaluation.

**What result would count as failure?**

Examples include:

- failure to reproduce a declared equation in code;
- unbounded or numerically unstable evolution outside declared conditions;
- loss of protected structure during reconstruction;
- failure to distinguish a fluent imitation from a high-fidelity reconstruction;
- failure of attractors to persist under stated perturbations;
- failure to transfer across declared scales or procedures;
- worse performance than appropriate baselines;
- receipt or provenance claims that do not match observed execution;
- dependence on undisclosed assumptions that determine the result.

Negative results should cause revision, rejection, mutation, or explicit narrowing—not rhetorical reinterpretation.

## MythMath

MythMath is the authored mathematical corpus and executable developmental layer of BLOOMCORE. It binds symbolic compression, formal mathematics, computation, falsification, lineage, and reconstruction into one full circuit.

It is not mathematics added after a mythic story, and it is not decorative language added after code.

### 15. What is a MythMath card?

A rebuilt MythMath card is an executable scientific specification or developmental packet containing, where applicable:

- canonical mathematics;
- defined variables and operators;
- assumptions;
- interpretable inputs and outputs;
- measurable behavior;
- failure criteria;
- implementation guidance;
- a code stub.

These are not ordinary knowledge cards or prompt cards.

### 16. How does a MythMath card become part of BLOOMCORE?

The intended progression is:

```text
canonical mathematics
→ implementation scaffold
→ executable module
→ tests
→ runtime evidence
→ deterministic receipt
→ bounded integration into BLOOMCORE
```

Extraction is not implementation, compilation is not validation, and a receipt is not proof of scientific truth.

**Is the mathematics or the code the source of truth?**

The canonical mathematics is the source of truth for the mathematical claim. Code is one executable expression of that mathematics.

The code must pass implementation-fidelity tests showing that it actually computes the declared equation. If code and equation disagree, running code does not silently redefine the mathematics.

**Are all MythMath cards validated or production-ready?**

No. Cards may be seeds, experimental specifications, active modules, falsified objects, mutations, or retired lineage.

Cards may be grouped into modules, tissues, organs, or system-level functions, but organizational placement does not establish empirical validity.

### 17. What are Full-Circuit MythMath and Full Fire JAX?

Full-Circuit MythMath requires symbolic, mathematical, computational, falsification, and lineage surfaces to develop together.

Full Fire JAX is the preferred implementation doctrine for compute-critical differentiable dynamics: real equations, native operators, explicit state, tests, reference calculations where useful, and declared failure conditions. ## ECA — Elemental Coherence Atlas

### 29. What is ECA?

ECA — Elemental Coherence Atlas is a bounded computational mapping, simulation, diagnostic, intervention-proposal, validation, and evidence system within BLOOMCORE.

It translates declared roles, constraints, field states, temporal evidence, and candidate transitions into inspectable computational structures.

**What does “elemental” mean?**

“Elemental” began with role-based representations of chemical elements and expanded into an atlas grammar for functional roles, coupling tendencies, constraints, and interaction patterns.

Elemental roles are models and parameterizations. They are not permission to replace chemistry, physics, or experimental measurement with symbolic assignments.

### 30. How does ECA relate to World Engine, SWIM Brain, and BLOOMWAVE?

- **World Engine** supplies an embodiment chamber in which actions encounter simulated consequence, perturbation, and environmental response.
- **SWIM Brain** supplies cognitive and state dynamics that may perceive and respond within that environment.
- **BLOOMWAVE** supplies differentiated agents and roles.
- **ECA** observes, maps, perturbs, simulates, evaluates, and records bounded interactions among declared states and candidates.

ECA supports those layers without replacing them.

### 31. What do ECA receipts and flight recorders do?

They preserve accepted state transitions, parameters, provenance, stochastic choices, validation outcomes, and replay references.

They support audit and reconstructive replay. They do not certify truth merely because they are hash-linked.

**How does ECA handle contradiction and uncertainty?**

Valid ECA outputs may include uncertainty, insufficiency, contestation, hold, rejection, or falsification. Contradictory evidence should be preserved rather than averaged into a falsely confident scalar.

No single score, agent, detector, or receipt may independently define health, truth, identity, or malignancy.

### 32. What is the quantum executable analogue boundary?

ECA may translate concepts from quantum theory, field theory, quantum chemistry, geometry, and biological regulation into bounded, classically executable analogues.

Examples include:

- tunneling analogue: rare transition across a high-cost barrier;
- entanglement analogue: coupled state dependency;
- measurement analogue: computational commitment from alternatives to a recorded decision;
- excited-state analogue: elevated or metastable computational state;
- relaxation analogue: return toward a lower-cost or more stable basin.

> ECA may not claim literal quantum authority without corresponding evidence.

### 33. How is ECA different from ordinary optimization or control software?

ECA is designed to preserve more than an objective score. It can require:

- multi-objective diagnostics;
- attractor and transfer analysis;
- uncertainty and contradiction retention;
- explicit role boundaries;
- temporal validity;
- non-coercion constraints;
- lineage and provenance;
- reconstructive receipts;
- separation among proposal, evaluation, authorization, execution, and recording.

Whether ECA outperforms established methods remains a benchmark question, not an assumed fact.
