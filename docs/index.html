03 / EVIDENCE

Novelty is not proof.
Execution is not validation.

  1. 01Define

    Domain, state variables, operators, assumptions, and boundaries.

  2. 02Implement

    Typed interfaces, explicit state, deterministic fixtures, and failure behavior.

  3. 03Measure

    Estimators, baselines, uncertainty, perturbations, and rejection rules.

  4. 04Record

    Provenance, accepted transitions, parameters, and replay references.

  5. 05Challenge

    Ablations, negative controls, contradiction tests, and independent review.

“The framework should be judged through its definitions, equations, assumptions, executable implementations, measurable outputs, validation tests, and explicit failure criteria—not through the novelty of its name.”
