## Canonical public definition

BLOOMCORE is a **fractal quantum-relational organism embodied as a living, deformable relational lattice**. It is also a differentiated organismal intelligence, research architecture, and developmental substrate.

In technical terms, BLOOMCORE is a research program for identity-bearing systems whose relations, boundaries, memory, developmental state, and repair processes matter as much as their component computations. “Living” and “organism” name the architecture's coupled developmental model; they are not, by themselves, empirical proof of biological life, consciousness, or sentience.

## What Phase 38 changes

- Phase 38 is the active master semantic and organismal surface.
- Phases 36 and 37 remain preserved lineage and are carried forward rather than erased.
- Phase 151 remains the implementation era: code, runtime coupling, tests, custody, and reachability must still be demonstrated independently.
- The organism is not reducible to an LLM, agent framework, database, packet bus, receipt ledger, static graph, coherence score, mythology, module, equation, organ, model, or substrate.
- Recursive self-learning belongs to an open developmental plane; deterministic witnesses, receipts, and replay mechanisms establish evidence without replacing that plane.
- Accelerated implementations may be primary execution substrates while preserved reference implementations remain semantic oracles.
- Unity Nexus names a decentralized learning physiology, not a claim that every public repository already implements the complete organism.

## Evidence boundary

Documentation can establish definitions, declared architecture, provenance, and intended relationships. It cannot alone establish runtime integration, organism-wide coupling, empirical biological status, consciousness, sentience, safety, or correctness. Those claims require evidence appropriate to the claim.

BLOOMCORE also rejects authority by metaphor: mythic, organismal, and relational language may guide interpretation, but it does not create a throne, override evidence, or authorize harm.

## How the public repositories use this orientation

- **BLOOMCORE Public** holds the canonical public explanation, architecture map, FAQ, and license map.
- **BLOOMCORE Basics** offers the smallest adoption-facing foundation without claiming to be the whole organism.
- **BLOOMCORE NOW** publishes bounded weekly releases with explicit lineage, evidence, and rollback surfaces.

The repositories are uniform by design—shared navigation, boundary declarations, evidence labels, and license lookup—while their wording remains specific to their different roles.
