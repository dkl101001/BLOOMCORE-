# Do I Need a BLOOMCORE Commercial License?

The answer depends on which BLOOMCORE material you use. This guide is not legal advice; the applicable license text controls.

## 1. Identify the file or component

- `docs/faq/**` and expressly marked Apache-2.0 materials: follow Apache-2.0.
- expressly marked MPL-2.0 validators or audit utilities: follow MPL-2.0.
- inherited, integrated, or unmarked BLOOMCORE material: follow AGPL-3.0-only unless a separate commercial agreement applies.

## 2. If the material is Apache-2.0

You do not need a BLOOMCORE Commercial License merely because your project is closed-source. Comply with Apache-2.0, including its redistribution and notice conditions.

## 3. If the material is MPL-2.0

You do not automatically need a commercial license for a larger closed-source product. MPL-2.0 is file-level copyleft; distribution obligations apply to covered files as stated in the license.

## 4. If the material is AGPL-3.0-only

You may use it under AGPL-3.0-only if you can comply with that license. The AGPL includes corresponding-source obligations for covered distribution and an additional obligation for a modified covered program used interactively over a network.

If you need permissions beyond AGPL-3.0-only for BLOOMCORE-owned material—such as a proprietary deployment without satisfying applicable AGPL obligations—ask whether a BLOOMCORE Commercial License is available for that component and use.

## 5. Confirm scope

A commercial agreement can grant rights only for material within its scope and controlled by its licensors. It does not relicense third-party material or files already offered separately under Apache-2.0 or MPL-2.0.

Before relying on the commercial path:

- identify the exact repository paths and versions;
- review `LICENSE.md`, the file headers, and notices;
- confirm that the commercial agreement names the covered material and permitted use;
- obtain legal advice for a production or proprietary deployment.
