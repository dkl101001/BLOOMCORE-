\subsection{Table Usage Guidance}
When reporting \RSS, authors should (i) include a decomposition table, (ii) indicate inapplicable metrics, (iii) describe instrumentation concisely, and (iv) avoid comparing absolute \RSS\ values across benchmarks with substantially different artifact richness.
