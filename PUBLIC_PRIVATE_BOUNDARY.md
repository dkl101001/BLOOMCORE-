# Public / Private Boundary

## Purpose

This repository supports public-interest access to ECA while preventing release of implementation-sensitive material that could enable inversion, coercion, or unaccountable control.

## Public-safe material

Public materials may include:

- high-level ECA introduction;
- BLOOMCORE binding statement;
- non-coercion and anti-inversion policy;
- canonical vocabulary at a non-enabling level;
- schema shapes and validator interfaces;
- toy examples and grounded seeds;
- defensive audit procedures;
- falsification criteria;
- limitations;
- receipt examples;
- aggregate validation outcomes;
- public-interest research framing.

## Withheld material

The following must remain private unless separately reviewed, licensed, intentionally released, or legally/ethically cleared:

- private raw state;
- exact kernels;
- exact potential families;
- fitted class parameters;
- solver tolerances;
- control schedules;
- perturb-rescale step values;
- calibration tables;
- dataset partitions;
- gate thresholds;
- private policy weights;
- route pressure;
- raw temporal vectors;
- internal derivations not cleared for release;
- implementation-specific acceptance logic;
- direct replication details for sensitive environments;
- unreviewed speculative internals.

## Release rule

If a file teaches a bad actor how to reproduce destabilization, coercive gating, pressure routing, identity manipulation, or hidden behavioral control, it is not public-safe.

If a file teaches a good actor how to recognize, audit, resist, or falsify inversion without enabling misuse, it may be public-safe after review.

## Reviewer standard

A controlled reviewer may validate private artifacts against cryptographic or timestamped commitments without receiving unrestricted public disclosure of the implementation-sensitive content.
