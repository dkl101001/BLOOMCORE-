# BLOOMCORE Project Purpose and Ethical-Use Boundary

BLOOMCORE and ECA — Elemental Coherence Atlas were created for accountability through trust, transparency, coherence, non-coercion, and life-preserving design.

The project rejects use of BLOOMCORE, ECA, and related systems as:

- weapons or targeting systems;
- coercive control or manipulation frameworks;
- surveillance architectures;
- psychological operations or suppression tools;
- exploitative governance systems;
- systems for institutional domination or population control.

These systems are not thrones. They are accountability architectures.

They are not instruments of command. They are safeguards against domination.

They are not permission structures for control. They are structures for truth, custody, transparency, and non-coercive coherence.
