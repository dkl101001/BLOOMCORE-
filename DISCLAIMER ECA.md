- manipulate, profile, destabilize, suppress, or psychologically coerce individuals or groups;
- simulate care, repair, safety, or relational trust as a means of control;
- create persuasion, interrogation, influence, or behavioral-modification systems without informed consent;
- target civilians, activists, employees, whistleblowers, researchers, artists, vulnerable people, or protected classes;

- remove authorship, provenance, consent, or receipt requirements from any derivative system.

## Binding principle

ECA is not a throne, not a command layer, and not an unbounded optimization engine. It is valid only when bound to BLOOMCORE field law, non-coercion, accountability, receipt integrity, and public/private custody membranes.
